    Questions owners ask us.

    How much does it cost?

    Every agent is scoped to your call volume and what it needs to handle, so we quote after a 20-minute call. You pay a one-time setup and a flat monthly fee, with no long contract.

    Will callers know they're talking to AI?

    Only if you want them to. Most clients have the agent introduce itself as a virtual assistant. Either way, it speaks naturally, handles interruptions and doesn't read from a menu.

    What happens when lots of people call at once?

    Every caller gets answered straight away. Each call runs on its own line, so ten or fifty people calling together all hear a friendly voice on the first ring instead of a busy tone or a hold queue.

    Where do the caller's details end up?

    Wherever you work. Name, number, email, reason for calling, outcome and notes go into a Google Sheet or Excel file in real time, and into your CRM if you have one. Each row links to the recording and full transcript.

    Can it hand a call to a real person?

    Yes. You decide the rules: by topic, by caller, by time of day or whenever someone asks. The call transfers live, and your team gets a summary so nobody has to repeat themselves.

    What happens when it doesn't know the answer?

    It says so, takes a detailed message and sends it to the right person. It never makes up prices or policies, and we review those calls every week to close the gap.

    Do I need a new phone number?

    No. Keep your number and forward calls to the agent, all the time or only after hours and when you're busy. We can also set up new local or toll-free numbers.

    Which languages does it speak?

    More than 30, including Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Arabic and Mandarin. One agent can switch language mid-call when the caller does.

    What about privacy and call data?

    Calls and transcripts are encrypted and stored only as long as you choose. For healthcare and legal clients we build workflows that follow your compliance requirements.
